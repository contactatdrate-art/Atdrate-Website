Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026.

What we collect

When you submit an application or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly — name, company, email, website, business context, and the answers to our application questions. We also collect basic analytics (page views, anonymized device data) to improve the site.

How we use it

We use the information you share solely to evaluate fit, prepare for our qualification call, and communicate with you about your application. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we protect it

Application data is stored in access-controlled internal systems and is reviewed only by Atdrate partners and analysts directly involved in your evaluation. Sensitive financial information shared during engagements is governed by a separate NDA.

Your rights

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of any personal data you've shared with us at any time by emailing hello@atdrate.com.

Cookies

This site uses essential cookies for navigation and minimal first-party analytics. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Contact
