Frequently Asked

Common questions about working with a business consultancy

How much does a business consultant cost in India?

ATDRATE offers three engagement models: a 4-week Consultancy Audit, project-based sprints of 3–6 months, and retained 12-month growth partnerships. Pricing depends on the scope of engagement across our four pillars — Marketing, HR, Finance, and Operations.

What does a business growth consultancy do?

A business growth consultancy like ATDRATE embeds into your leadership team to diagnose structural inefficiencies across Marketing, HR, Finance, and Operations — then executes improvements across all four pillars simultaneously, rather than fixing one function in isolation.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. ATDRATE works with founders from early-stage businesses through Series A scale-ups. We tailor our engagement model — audit, project, or retained — to match your current stage and budget.

Is ATDRATE based in Mumbai?

Yes, ATDRATE is headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, and works with founders and leadership teams across India.
